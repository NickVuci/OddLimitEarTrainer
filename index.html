Game effects

Turn on any mix of playbacks; each round uses one of them at random, and at least one stays on. Tap a tone to hear it on a major third. On the path, each step runs first with every round on 220 Hz, then again with the root changing each round (varying the root spreads that over two octaves), and varying the tone colour rotates through every tone. Answer tiles apply to the full-limit stages (Auto shows 8); each ratio stage sets its own answers as you go through its steps. Flute and Glassy have few harmonics, so higher-limit locks are harder to hear in them.

Pairs you mix up

Duel length

A pair lands here when you pick one for the other. In a duel only those two play, equally often, with the root changing each round; win it by naming them right that many times in a row, and the pair comes off the list. Duels don't change your place on the path.

Progress

Unlocking the whole path also turns on developer mode: a bar under the path for jumping to any section of a stage, or marking the current section complete. Restarting the path turns it off. Jumping back into a stage you had passed locks the stages after it again; choosing "Mastered" in the bar restores them.
